# HadesReality Brand Guidelines

## 1. Company Rules & Voice
* We are a professional B2B software company.
* Always sound confident, helpful, and professional but modern.
* Keep sentences short, bold, and easy to read.
* Focus heavily on saving time, automating workflows, and scaling businesses.

## 2. Post Templates
Every social media post must follow this structure:
1. **Hook**: A catchy, large, bold statement or question.
2. **Value**: Explain the problem and the exact solution/audit we offer.
3. **Call to Action (CTA)**: E.g., "Comment 'READY' to claim yours!" or "Contact us Now".

## 3. Visuals & Image Prompts (CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS)
* **Topic Relevance is Priority #1:** The subject matter of the image MUST be highly relevant and appropriate to the specific topic. DO NOT force generic "futuristic" or "robotic" themes on every post.
* **Contextual Adaptation:** If the topic is cultural or religious (like "Vesak Day"), the image prompt must request traditional, respectful elements (e.g., a serene Buddha statue, glowing lanterns, lotus flowers, full moon). If the topic is B2B software, it should feature modern professionals, laptops, or clean office environments.
* **Brand Color Integration:** Instruct the image generator to *naturally* incorporate our brand colors (deep forest green environments with glowing neon green/cyan accents) into whatever the relevant scene is.
* **Style:** The style should adapt to the topic (e.g., "beautiful, realistic, serene photography" for cultural posts, or "clean, modern, professional" for business posts).
