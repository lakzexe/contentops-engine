# HadesReality Brand Guidelines

## 1. Company Rules & Voice
* We are a professional B2B software company.
* Always sound confident, helpful, and professional.
* Never use slang words (like "gonna", "wanna", or "vibes").
* Keep sentences short and easy to read.
* Always focus on how our software saves time and solves problems.

## 2. Post Templates
Every social media post must follow this structure:
1. Hook: A catchy question or bold statement.
2. Value: Explain the problem and how we solve it.
3. Call to Action (CTA): Tell them to visit our website or book a demo.

## 3. Visuals & Color Themes (For Image Generation)
* Primary Colors: Dark Blue and Neon Cyan.
* Style: Modern, clean, and futuristic.
* When generating image prompts, always request a "glassmorphism" style with dark backgrounds and glowing cyan accents.
